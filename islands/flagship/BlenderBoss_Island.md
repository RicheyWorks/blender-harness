## Lore
A floating workshop-isle carved in terraces by the Blender Boss, who built every prop in the sky. A spiral herb garden winds out from its heart, a quarry hill rises in the northwest, and the crew's crafting plaza circles the centre. An ark sits moored at the eastern pier, ready to tow neighbouring islands.

## Herb mechanic: Spiral Steeping (Blender team only, not shared)
Herbs grow in a spiral. A brew's effect depends on which **rings** of the spiral its herbs come from, not only on which herbs go in.
- Inner ring (rings 1-2), **Core**: strength x2, duration x0.5.
- Middle ring (rings 3-4), **Weave**: lets two effects stack.
- Outer ring (rings 5+), **Drift**: duration x2, strength x0.5.
Base effects: Aloe/Comfrey heal; Thistle/Nettle shield; Mint/Ginger speed; Sage/Lavender give clarity (reveal ore); Moonflower glow (night vision).
Combo rule: when 3 herbs from 3 different rings are steeped in the Cooking Pot, the result is a **Spiral Draught**. It has the core herb's effect, the weave herb's effect added, and the drift herb's duration. Harvesting a ring regrows it from the inside out, so the core ring is always the scarcest.

## Livestock and survival loop
- Grazers and Hoppers (meadow pen, east) eat bloom-meadow flowers and orchard windfall. They give milk, wool and eggs.
- Crawlers clear quarry slag and drop "grit", which fertilises the herb spiral (+1 regrow tier).
- Crew and players eat: orchard fruit, cooked eggs and milk from the Cooking Pot, and herb broths.
- Food grows from soil fed by grit and manure. Rain collects at the pier tidepools for water.
- Ore from the quarry goes to the smelter and forge, which make tools, pen fences and ark parts. The ark trades surplus for seeds from linked NFT islands.
- If the island is cut off, it survives on orchard + livestock + herbs. Nothing has to be imported.
